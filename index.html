ABOUT SHIGUANG CARDS
這一套牌卡，從校園出發，也從孩子的創意開始。
光正國中視覺藝術課程帶領學生，
將校園景物與生活記憶融入創作，
透過攝影與 iPad 數位繪圖，
將熟悉的校園風景轉化為獨具特色的牌卡圖像。
These cards began on our campus and grew from students' creativity.
Through photography and digital drawing on iPad,
familiar campus scenes were transformed into a collection of meaningful cards.
